Dashboards fail in a specific way: they show everything, and explain nothing. The fix is editorial before it is technical.

## One question per chart

If a viewer needs a paragraph to understand a chart, the chart is doing two jobs. Split it. Dashboards are conversations, not archives.

## Color with intent

Reserve saturated color for the series that answers the question; render everything else in quiet gray. When everything is highlighted, nothing is.

## Motion for continuity

Use animation to preserve continuity between states — a short transition that shows where a bar went. Never for decoration.
